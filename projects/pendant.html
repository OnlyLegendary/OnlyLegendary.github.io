The project

What went into it.

The goal was to design an electronic wearable as a single object. Rather than treating the housing and electronics separately, I designed the pendant geometry around an internal PCB with LEDs mounted directly on the board. The CAD model shows the skyline face, outer enclosure and hanging loop.

  • Modeled the round pendant housing, face details and necklace loop in 3D CAD.
  • Designed the concept around a custom PCB with integrated LEDs.
  • Considered the space needed for components within the wearable enclosure.
  • Worked across PCB and mechanical geometry instead of treating them as unrelated pieces.
  • Produced a visual render to communicate the intended physical design.

This is a CAD and electronics concept. No physical manufactured pendant or measured lighting performance is shown here.
